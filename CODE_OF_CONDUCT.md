# Code of Conduct

OpenKnowledge is a learning project built by and for a broad community of
contributors and learners. Everyone participating is expected to help keep
this a welcoming, harassment-free space.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions, issues, and reviews.
- Assume good faith; critique the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and first-time contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open a private
report through the repository's contact method listed in `SECURITY.md`, or
flag it to a maintainer directly. Reports will be handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, or other
interactions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily
or permanently restrict access for repeated or severe violations.
